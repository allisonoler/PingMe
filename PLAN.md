PLAN

Goal:
- mobile app in the ready to deploy phase ideally, settle for web app if it dont work

Features:
- users have friends and can send out a ping containing info about what it is that they're doing or planning on doing (studying, sports, gym, party, etc.) and their location and how long they will be there
- friends receive ping notification if they have opted to receive pings either from that person or about that particular type of activity or at that certain time (default is you just always get pinged by your friends for everything they do)
- potentially add a way to ping all of your friends' friends for open invite sort of activities like a party or sm
- map to see all active pings from friends
- interface to search up new friends by username
- interface to manage friends and which pings to receive from who
- ping groups to only ping a certain group of your friends
- character customization (think snapchat map)
- ability to accept pings, shows the ping sender the friends that have accepted and total friends that accepted the ping
